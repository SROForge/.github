## About SROForge

SROForge brings client development, server extensions, security, licensing, deployment, and web-based management together under one professional platform.

Our goal is to provide server owners with reliable, maintainable, and carefully engineered solutions across the complete vSRO environment.

## Platform

* **Client Engineering** — Custom interfaces, gameplay systems, automation tools, and client-side integrations.
* **Server Development** — GameServer and ShardManager extensions built on a dedicated server development framework.
* **Security & Licensing** — Controlled access, server-locked licensing, integrity verification, and deployment protection.
* **Web Control** — Centralized accounts, licenses, products, downloads, updates, and customer access.
* **Continuous Development** — New systems, improvements, testing, and long-term platform expansion.
